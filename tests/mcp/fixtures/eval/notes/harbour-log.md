# Gull Rock harbour log

An invented record for the MCP evaluation. Every name, place and date in it is fictional.

## History

The Gull Rock light was first lit in 1871, when the keeper's cottage still had a thatched roof and the lamp burned colza oil.

## Lantern room

During the restoration the cracked panes of the lantern room glass were replaced with amber glass, which the harbour board chose to soften the beam for the fishing boats coming home.

## Fog signal

The fog signal failed in the storm season. Keeper Ada Merrow repaired the fog signal diaphragm by hand and logged the repair in the day book.

## Tide gauge

The spring tide gauge is checked twice a year against the brass datum plate set into the harbour wall.
