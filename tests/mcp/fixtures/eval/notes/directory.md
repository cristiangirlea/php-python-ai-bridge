# Harbour directory

An invented record for the MCP evaluation. Every name and place in it is fictional.

## Yards and depots

Pellow and Daughters keep their yard at Saltash Cove, beside the old lime kiln.

Hesketh Fuels runs its depot from the old rail sidings at Penmarth.

Brannock Ropeworks has its rope walk at Wendle Quay, where the river narrows.

Tollin Marine keeps its yard at Carrowby, two miles up the estuary from Gull Rock.

Quiller and Sons keep their forge at Ashby Mill and send a smith out when one is wanted.

## Harbour office

The harbour office opens on weekday mornings. Letters for the keepers are left there and taken out with the relief boat.
