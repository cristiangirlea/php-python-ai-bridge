# Gull Rock keepers

An invented record for the MCP evaluation. Every name, place and date in it is fictional.

## Watches

Two keepers share the night in four-hour watches. The keeper on watch climbs to the lantern at dusk, trims the wick and winds the clockwork that turns the lens.

At dawn the lantern is put out, the lens is curtained against the sun, and the night's weather is copied into the day book.

## Roster

Keeper Tom Ellery came from the Penmarth pilot boats and stayed eleven years. He rebuilt the landing stage crane after the winter of 1896 and greased its winch every month.

Keeper Nell Tresize kept the station's accounts. When the spring of the lens clockwork broke she repaired the clockwork herself with a spring cut from a clock in the cottage.

Keeper Jory Pascoe joined as an assistant, later took charge of the stores, and wrote the stores book in a hand the inspectors praised.

## Relief

The relief boat comes out from the harbour steps every second Thursday, weather allowing, with letters, fresh water and the keepers' pay. In a long gale the keepers ration the water and wait.

## Visitors

Visitors may climb to the lantern gallery on summer afternoons with a keeper beside them. They are asked not to touch the lens, and to sign the visitors' book on the way down.
