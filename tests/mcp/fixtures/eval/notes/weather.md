# Gull Rock weather book

An invented record for the MCP evaluation. Every name, place and date in it is fictional.

## The great storm

During the great storm of 1903 the light went dark for three nights when spray flooded the lamp room, and it shone again on the fourth evening after the keepers bailed it out.

## Ice

In the hard winter of 1917 sleet crusted the lantern gallery rail, and the keepers chipped it clear each morning before cleaning the glass.

## Fog

Fog is logged by the hour. The worst year on record was 1911, with forty-one days of fog between October and March.

## Wind

Gales are logged when the harbour flag stands straight out. The anemometer on the gallery rail was added in 1924 and is read at every change of watch.

## Tides

Spring tides are logged against the harbour wall at high and low water. The highest ever logged came with the storm of 1903 and reached the second step of the cottage.
