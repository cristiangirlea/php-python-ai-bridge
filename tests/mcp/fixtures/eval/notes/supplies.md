# Gull Rock supplies

An invented record for the MCP evaluation. Every company and place in it is fictional.

## Deliveries

The copper fittings for the lantern gallery were delivered by Tollin Marine in crates stamped with a blue anchor.

Paraffin for the reserve lamp comes by cart from the Hesketh Fuels depot every second month.

## Suppliers

Tollin Marine keeps its yard at Carrowby, two miles up the estuary from Gull Rock.

Hesketh Fuels runs its depot from the old rail sidings at Penmarth.
