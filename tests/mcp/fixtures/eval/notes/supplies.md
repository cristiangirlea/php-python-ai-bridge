# Gull Rock supplies

An invented record for the MCP evaluation. Every company and place in it is fictional.

## Deliveries

The copper fittings for the lantern gallery were delivered by Tollin Marine in crates stamped with a blue anchor.

The iron fittings for the landing stage were delivered by Pellow and Daughters on a spring tide, lashed to the deck of their barge.

Paraffin for the reserve lamp comes by cart from the Hesketh Fuels depot every second month.

New rope for the boat davits was delivered by Brannock Ropeworks, coiled in tarred sacking.

## Stores

Stores are ordered each quarter and paid for on delivery. Spent wicks, broken panes and empty paraffin tins go back on the returning cart.

Spare mantles, lens cloths and a tin of rouge for polishing the lantern brass are kept in the oil store.
