# Gull Rock inspections

An invented record for the MCP evaluation. Every name, place and date in it is fictional.

## Spring inspection

The inspector comes out in April with the relief boat. He begins at the lantern, checks that the lens turns true, and times one full turn against his watch.

He then reads the day book, the stores book and the weather book, and signs each at its last entry.

## Autumn inspection

In October the inspector looks at the building rather than the light: the roof, the gutters, the landing stage and the rails of the lantern gallery.

Any defect goes into the defects book with a date by which it must be put right, and the keepers sign beneath it.

## Signals

The inspector also sounds the fog signal for one minute and listens from the landing stage. A signal that cannot be heard there is written up as a defect.

## Reports

The inspector's report goes to the harbour board, which meets once a month in the harbour office and answers each defect in writing.
